TWICE Timeline

< Back to TWICE

2015 — Formation & debut

JYP Entertainment forms TWICE through the survival reality show SIXTEEN. Originally planned as a seven-member group, producer Park Jin-young opts to expand the final lineup to nine, adding Momo and Tzuyu. TWICE debuts on October 20, 2015 with the mini album The Story Begins.

2016 — Breakthrough

Page Two and its single "Cheer Up" turn TWICE into one of the top rookie groups in K-pop. "TT," from Twicecoaster: Lane 1, becomes a cultural phenomenon later that year, cementing the group's signature bright, gestural choreography style.

2017–2019 — Consolidation & global reach

TWICE continues a steady run of comebacks ("Signal," "What is Love?," "Fancy," "Feel Special") while expanding into Japan and building an increasingly international fanbase.

2020–2022 — Full albums & maturity

TWICE releases Eyes Wide Open, their first full-length Korean album, followed by Formula of Love: O+T=<3 and Between 1&2, reflecting a more mature sound and greater involvement by members in songwriting and production.

2023–2025 — Solo era & anniversary

Members increasingly pursue solo projects alongside group activities — including Jihyo's ZONE and Chaeyoung's LIL FANTASY — while the group releases Ready To Be, With YOU-th, Strategy, and This Is For. In 2025, TWICE marks ten years since debut with the release #TWICE5.

---

This timeline covers major milestones and is not a complete history of releases, awards, or activities.
