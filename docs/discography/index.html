TWICE Discography

< Back to TWICE

TWICE has released a long run of Korean-language EPs and albums, plus a parallel line of Japanese releases, since their 2015 debut. Below is a selection of milestone releases rather than a full catalog.

Debut & breakout (2015-2017)

  • The Story Begins (2015) - debut mini album, lead single "Like Ooh-Ahh"
  • Page Two (2016) - includes the breakout hit "Cheer Up"
  • Twicecoaster: Lane 1 (2016) - lead single "TT"
  • Signal (2017)

Global growth (2018-2021)

  • What is Love? (2018)
  • Fancy You (2019) - lead single "Fancy"
  • Feel Special (2019)
  • Eyes Wide Open (2020, full album)
  • Formula of Love: O+T=<3 (2021, full album)

Recent era (2022-2025)

  • Between 1&2 (2022, full album)
  • Ready To Be (2023)
  • With YOU-th (2024)
  • Strategy (2024)
  • This Is For (2025)
  • #TWICE5 (2025) - anniversary release marking a decade since debut, featuring the track "Enemy"
