# TWICE Members

[< Back to TWICE](/)

TWICE has nine members, each occupying a distinct role in the group and its performances.

## Nayeon
**Position:** Lead Vocalist, Lead Dancer, Center

The oldest member of TWICE, Nayeon is often called the group's visual centerpiece and is known for her bright stage presence and expressive vocals. She has also built out a solo career alongside her group activities.

## Jeongyeon
**Position:** Lead Vocalist

Known for her powerful belting voice and playful, tomboyish charm, Jeongyeon is one of the group's key vocal anchors on ballads and high-energy tracks alike.

## Momo
**Position:** Main Dancer, Sub Vocalist, Sub Rapper

Born in Japan, Momo is regarded as one of TWICE's strongest dancers, frequently taking center position during choreography-heavy sections of performances.

## Sana
**Position:** Sub Vocalist

Also from Japan, Sana is known for her bubbly personality and distinctive vocal tone, and remains one of the group's most recognizable personalities in variety appearances.

## Jihyo
**Position:** Leader, Main Vocalist

Jihyo trained for roughly a decade before debuting and was chosen by her fellow members as TWICE's leader. 

## Mina
**Position:** Main Dancer, Sub Vocalist

Born in the United States and raised in Japan, Mina is known for her graceful, elegant dance style and calm stage presence, bringing a distinct artistic sensibility to the group.

## Dahyun
**Position:** Lead Rapper, Sub Vocalist

Scouted after a solo dance performance in middle school, Dahyun brings energy and comic timing to TWICE's rap lines and variety appearances.

## Chaeyoung
**Position:** Main Rapper, Sub Vocalist

A visual artist as well as a musician, Chaeyoung designed TWICE's official logo and has pursued her own painting and drawing projects alongside music.

## Tzuyu
**Position:** Lead Dancer, Sub Vocalist, Maknae

The youngest member and the group's designated visual, Tzuyu is from Taiwan and was added to the final lineup partly on the strength of audience response during _SIXTEEN_.
