# TWICE Timeline

[< Back to TWICE](/)

## 2015 — Formation & debut

JYP Entertainment forms TWICE through the survival reality show _SIXTEEN_. Originally planned as a seven-member group, producer Park Jin-young opts to expand the final lineup to nine, adding Momo and Tzuyu. TWICE debuts on October 20, 2015 with the mini album _The Story Begins_.

## 2016 — Breakthrough

_Page Two_ and its single "Cheer Up" turn TWICE into one of the top rookie groups in K-pop. "TT," from _Twicecoaster: Lane 1_, becomes a cultural phenomenon later that year, cementing the group's signature bright, gestural choreography style.

## 2017–2019 — Consolidation & global reach

TWICE continues a steady run of comebacks ("Signal," "What is Love?," "Fancy," "Feel Special") while expanding into Japan and building an increasingly international fanbase.

## 2020–2022 — Full albums & maturity

TWICE releases _Eyes Wide Open_, their first full-length Korean album, followed by _Formula of Love: O+T=&lt;3_ and _Between 1&2_, reflecting a more mature sound and greater involvement by members in songwriting and production.

## 2023–2025 — Solo era & anniversary

Members increasingly pursue solo projects alongside group activities — including Jihyo's _ZONE_ and Chaeyoung's _LIL FANTASY_ — while the group releases _Ready To Be_, _With YOU-th_, _Strategy_, and _This Is For_. In 2025, TWICE marks ten years since debut with the release _#TWICE5_.

---

_This timeline covers major milestones and is not a complete history of releases, awards, or activities._
