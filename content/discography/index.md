# TWICE Discography

[< Back to TWICE](/)

TWICE has released a long run of Korean-language EPs and albums, plus a parallel line of Japanese releases, since their 2015 debut. Below is a selection of milestone releases rather than a full catalog.

## Debut & breakout (2015-2017)

- _The Story Begins_ (2015) - debut mini album, lead single "Like Ooh-Ahh"
- _Page Two_ (2016) - includes the breakout hit "Cheer Up"
- _Twicecoaster: Lane 1_ (2016) - lead single "TT"
- _Signal_ (2017)

## Global growth (2018-2021)

- _What is Love?_ (2018)
- _Fancy You_ (2019) - lead single "Fancy"
- _Feel Special_ (2019)
- _Eyes Wide Open_ (2020, full album)
- _Formula of Love: O+T=&lt;3_ (2021, full album)

## Recent era (2022-2025)

- _Between 1&2_ (2022, full album)
- _Ready To Be_ (2023)
- _With YOU-th_ (2024)
- _Strategy_ (2024)
- _This Is For_ (2025)
- _#TWICE5_ (2025) - anniversary release marking a decade since debut, featuring the track "Enemy"
